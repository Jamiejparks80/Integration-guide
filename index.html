The tenancy lifecycle

Overview

Muuvo handles the change of occupier for every tenancy you send: notifying the council and the water and energy suppliers, offering tenants broadband and energy, and switching the property onto a void tariff when tenants leave. Your job is to send each stage at the right moment. We queue everything and release it at the right time.

  1. References passed
    Send the pre-tenancyPOST /tenancies as soon as references are passed, whether move-in is days or months away.
  2. ≤ 2 hours
    Utility and council data availableSuppliers, meter serial numbers and MPAN/MPRN are added to the property, usually instantly.
  3. Move-in
    Tenants move inComplete the check-in inventory and take meter readings.
  4. Target +5 days
    Send the move-in readingsPOST /tenancies/{id}/start ideally within five days of move-in. The longer readings wait, the more likely billing issues become.
  5. Notice given
    Send the end of tenancyPOST /tenancies/{id}/end as soon as notice is given, and more than five days before move-out. Most arrive around 60 days ahead.
  6. −5 days
    Void switch releasedMuuvo releases the property to British Gas, our void partner.
  7. Move-out
    Send closing readingsCall /end again with the closing readings and the tenants' forwarding address.
  8. +30 days
    Void period coveredZero standing charge for 30 days from move-out, plus a £30 bill credit for the landlord.
Order doesn't matter

An end of tenancy can reach Muuvo before the new tenancy at the same property, or the other way round. Send each piece of data as soon as you have it for the next change of occupancy, and we match them up.

Onboarding agencies

Setup

Each letting agency you work with is set up as its own company within Muuvo, with its own API key. Send each agency's tenancies using that agency's key. This is how we know which agency a tenancy belongs to, who to contact, and where agency-addressed void bills should go.

  • We set them up for you. Send us each agency's name, phone number, email and office address. We can do this one at a time as agencies join, or in bulk from a list.
  • Or create them yourself. If you'd rather create agencies from within your own platform, POST /companies is available as part of our V2 API. Ask your Muuvo contact for the V2 documentation and access.
Good to know

Void bills are never addressed to the integration partner. They go to the agency's correspondence address from its company profile, or to the landlord, depending on voidBillCorrespondenceAddress on each tenancy.

Authentication

Setup

Send the agency's key in the X-Muuvo-Key header on every request. Staging keys are issued at the start of your integration. Production keys are issued once your test data has been signed off.

curl https://agent.muuvostaging.com/services/tenancies/{tenancyId} \
  -H "X-Muuvo-Key: YOUR_AGENCY_KEY" \
  -H "Accept: application/json"

Building a tenancy

Workflow

Records build on each other. Create them in this order and keep each returned ID for the next step.

  1. LandlordPOST /landlords returns the landlord ID.
  2. PropertyPOST /properties with the landlordId and the property address. Returns the property ID, and we start matching meters and council straight away.
  3. Pre-tenancyPOST /tenancies with the propertyId, dates and service options. Returns the tenancy ID.
  4. TenantsPOST /tenancies/{tenancyId}/tenants once for each tenant.
  5. StartPOST /tenancies/{tenancyId}/start with the move-in readings.
  6. EndPOST /tenancies/{tenancyId}/end when notice is given, then again at move-out with closing readings.

    The void service
    Service
    
      
    Between tenancies, the landlord is usually liable for the bills. The void service takes the cost out of that gap. It only works if we know about the move-out early, which is why we ask for the end of tenancy as soon as notice is given.
    
      
    
        
    5 days beforeReleased to British Gas
    
        
    Move-out
    
        
    30 days afterZero standing charge and a £30 bill credit for the landlord
    
      
    
      
      
        
    • Five days before move-out, we release the property to British Gas, our void partner, so the supply goes live in the landlord's name.
      • 
        
    • From move-out day, the landlord gets a zero standing charge for 30 days and a £30 bill credit.
      • 
        
    • Send the move-out data more than five days before move-out. Most partners send it around 60 days ahead, as soon as notice is given.
      • 
        
    • Void bills under £30 and under 30 days are zeroed. This saves landlords and agents a lot of money, and commission is available on every switch.
      • 
        
    • We don't switch properties that don't qualify, for example where a new tenancy has already started or is about to.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Reading utility & council data
    Data
    
      
    When you create a property, Muuvo matches it against the national database and adds the council, water supplier, energy suppliers and meter details. You can read them back with either ID.
    
      
    
        
    
          
    CallReturns
    
          
            
    GET /properties/{id}Council, council tax band and cost, water supplier, UPRN; gas supplier, MPRN, meter serial, smart and prepay flags; electric supplier, MPAN, meter serial, smart, prepay and Economy 7 flags
    
            
    GET /tenancies/{id}Everything on the tenancy, plus electricity, gas and water meters with start and end readings, and the tenants
    
              
        
    
      
    
      
    Timing
    This data is usually available straight away. If a property doesn't match the national database, our team maps it to the correct meters manually. This takes up to two hours.
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    IDs and avoiding duplicates
    Data
    
      
    Each create call makes a new record, so posting the same tenancy twice creates two tenancies. To avoid this:
    
      
      
        
    • Store the landlord, property and tenancy IDs we return against your own records.
      • 
        
    • Use PUT with the stored ID for any change or correction. Don't post the record again.
      • 
        
    • Before creating a tenancy, check whether you already hold a Muuvo tenancy ID for it.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Status and reporting
    Reporting
    
      
      
        
    • Completion. When the council, water and energy have all been notified, we send a letting agent receipt to the agency. This is the last step for both a move-in and a move-out. A matching confirmation through the API is being planned.
      • 
        
    • Monthly reports. Everything is reported against the tenancy ID. You'll receive monthly reports covering the agencies in your portfolio, including services taken and commission.
      • 
        
    • Webhooks. There are no webhooks at present. Posting sales outcomes back against each tenancy is something we can look at with you.
      • 
      
    
      
    Not for integrators
    /addSales appears in the Swagger, but it's used by our call centre partners to report sales to Muuvo. You don't need it.

    Technical notes
    Reference
    
      
      
        
    • Dates. Send dates as DD-MM-YYYY. Dates come back as YYYY-MM-DD. We're confirming this applies to every field, including date of birth.
      • 
        
    • Date of birth casing. Send dateOfBirth. Home buyer and tenant lead records return it as dateofBirth.
      • 
        
    • Meter numbers. Store MPAN and MPRN as text, not numbers, so leading zeros are kept.
      • 
        
    • Rate limits. Rate limits apply. Your Muuvo contact will confirm the current limits.
      • 
        
    • Deletes. Tenancies, tenants, tenant leads and home buyers can be deleted. Landlords and properties can't be deleted through the API; ask us if you need one removed.
